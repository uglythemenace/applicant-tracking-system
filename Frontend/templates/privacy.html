Privacy notice

Last updated November 5, 2026

Information collected

When you apply, we collect your name and email address, the job you applied for, and the application materials you choose to provide. You may upload a PDF or DOCX résumé or provide a résumé link. Uploaded résumés are stored privately and shared with authorized hiring staff. A phone number is optional. If you provide one and choose text updates, we record that choice so we can send application messages.

How we use it

We use application information to review candidates, contact applicants about their applications, arrange interviews, prevent duplicate applications, and protect the service from misuse. Staff-only notes are used for hiring review and are not shown on the candidate tracking page.

Tracking your application

Your tracking code and the email address used to apply let you view your application status and any interview information shared with you. Keep the code private. The site does not require an account to apply or check a status.

Service providers and retention

The service uses hosting, database, and communications providers to operate the application process. Application records are kept while they are needed for hiring and related recordkeeping. The organization should set and follow a specific retention schedule and delete records when they are no longer needed.

Your choices and requests

You can withdraw an application from the tracking page while it is waiting for review, being reviewed, or at the interview stage. Providing a phone number and opting into text updates are optional. You can ask us to correct or delete application information by contacting the hiring team. The team may need to retain some records where required for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact details on our contact page.
